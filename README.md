# fruit-battlegrounds
Welcome to Fruit Battlegrounds, a fast-paced PvP arena where powerful fruits grant incredible abilities and every battle is a chance to grow stronger. Fight against players from around the world, unlock devastating abilities, discover powerful fruits, and level up to become the strongest warrior possible.

⚔️ LEVEL SYSTEM
Earn experience by battling opponents and mastering your fruit. Every victory brings you closer to greater power.

Maximum Level: 100,000

As your level increases, you'll unlock stronger skills, improve your combat abilities, and gain access to advanced techniques. Reaching Level 100,000 is the ultimate challenge for dedicated players.

🍎 FRUIT DEALER
Visit the Fruit Dealer to purchase powerful fruits and expand your collection.

• Fruit stock refreshes every 10 minutes
• Different fruits may appear each refresh
• Save your currency and check often for your desired fruit
• Rare fruits may take time to appear

🌴 FRUIT SPAWNS
Explore islands across the world to discover naturally spawned fruits.

• Random physical fruits spawn across islands every 30 minutes
• Spawn locations are randomized
• Fruits remain in the world for 15 minutes
• Uncollected fruits disappear when the timer expires
• Race against other players to find them first

🍏 AVAILABLE FRUITS

🌌 Gravity Fruit
Control the battlefield with overwhelming gravitational force. Pull enemies toward you, launch massive debris, create crushing shockwaves, and unleash devastating singularity attacks.

⚡ Lightning Fruit
Harness the speed and power of storms. Dash at incredible speeds, strike opponents with bolts of lightning, chain electricity between enemies, and dominate with rapid combos.

🌎 Quake Fruit
Wield earth-shattering destruction. Generate powerful shockwaves, crack the battlefield, and unleash massive attacks capable of launching enemies into the air.

🏆 BECOME THE STRONGEST
Battle skilled opponents, unlock stronger abilities, hunt for fruits across the islands, and continue growing stronger with every level. Master Gravity, Lightning, and Quake, develop powerful combos, and climb your way toward Level 100,000.

🛡️ ANTI-EXPLOIT SYSTEM

Fair gameplay is a top priority. Fruit Battlegrounds includes multiple anti-exploit systems designed to keep battles competitive and enjoyable.

• Automatic detection of suspicious movement and teleport exploits
• Protection against speed, fly, and noclip exploits
• Invalid damage and combat manipulation detection
• Auto-kick and auto-ban systems for severe violations
• Regular security updates to patch newly discovered exploits
• Server-side validation to reduce exploit abuse
• Reporting system for players who break the rules

Players who attempt to exploit the game risk losing access to their accounts. Train your skills, master your fruit, and earn your victories through fair gameplay.

The battlegrounds await. Which fruit will you master on your journey to becoming the strongest fighter alive?
